# City of Cockburn accessible parking

[parking.geojson](parking.geojson) contains 35 locations from the Perth OpenStreetMap extract retrieved on 27 November 2026. Coverage is partial. [BBBike Perth extract](https://download.bbbike.org/osm/bbbike/Perth/) · [© OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)

- **17 individual bay locations** use nodes or outlines tagged `amenity=parking_space` and `parking_space=disabled`.
- **Nine approximate locations** mark explicitly mapped groups of bays or street-side parking areas with an accessible capacity. The pin represents the group.
- **Nine car parks** use areas tagged with a positive `capacity:disabled`. A pin marks the car park, and the count comes from the source tag. The customer-only location is labelled in its record.

The snapshot uses OpenStreetMap nodes and ways inside the ABS Cockburn local government area. Suburb names use the ABS suburb boundaries and nearby street labels use OpenStreetMap. Each feature links to the original OpenStreetMap object. One car-park polygon duplicated by its mapped bay group, one car park with a capacity warning, and private venues without clear visitor access were omitted.

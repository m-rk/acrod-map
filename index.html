    About GeoJSON

    GeoJSON is a text file that stores map locations and details together. If your council already publishes a GIS layer, look for Download GeoJSON or export the layer from QGIS or ArcGIS in WGS 84.

    Starting with a spreadsheet or map? Give your AI the source and try this prompt.

    Convert this accessible-parking source to a WGS 84 GeoJSON FeatureCollection. Use [longitude, latitude] for supplied coordinates and preserve each bay or car park name, source reference and restriction. Leave unknown fields blank. Flag rows without coordinates for me to check on a map before export. Return plain JSON.

    For a PDF or an image, check each pin against a real map. Include the original source URL and its reuse licence when contributing.
